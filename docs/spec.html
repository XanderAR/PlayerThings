Product spec · working name

PlayRadar

player.gg inside Discord

A Discord app that brings player.gg's playtests, shelves and reviews into the servers where people already talk about games. Everything it posts is one click away from player.gg.

1

Another way to experience player.gg

Players browse playtests, show off their shelf, review games and track quests without leaving Discord.

2

A steady path to player.gg

Every card carries a player.gg button. Each one a player posts is seen by everyone in the channel, and sign-ups are credited to the server they came from.

How it works

Link once

Connect a player.gg account, plus Steam and PlayStation for playtime. One link works in every server.

Use it anywhere

Install it to your account for DMs and any server, or add it to a server so everyone there gets the shared features.

Results lead back

Profiles, games, playtests and reviews open on player.gg. Members only appear on a server's leaderboards if they opt in.

Someone runs a command→ The whole channel sees the card→ They click through to player.gg→ They link and run their own

Proof of concept, then the full version

Proof of concept

  • Steam linking through the bot's own page
  • /profile, /whoplays, /incommon, /chart from Steam data
  • /playtests and /radar from a hand-curated list of public playtest pages
  • Every button already points at player.gg
  • No special Discord permissions, no reading messages

With player.gg data

  • Sign in with player.gg; Steam and PlayStation come through player.gg's own sync
  • Shelf, reviews, XP and quests on cards
  • Live playtest feed with sign-ups credited per server
  • /review, /curator, /goty
  • Rich cards for pasted player.gg links
Concept for discussion, not an official player.gg product. Game titles are real examples; the playtests, studios, curator and players shown are made up.
